The story behind the counter

About Us

Veteran-owned, family-operated and built around quality meat, skilled service and the community that keeps coming back.

Welcome to The Butcher on Tucker.

We are a veteran- and family-owned butcher shop serving Vancleave and the Mississippi Gulf Coast. The goal has been straightforward from the beginning: offer excellent meat, know the product, and treat people like neighbors rather than transactions.

Our team focuses on the craft of butchery and on helping customers choose the right cut for the job. Whether you are planning a weeknight dinner, a cookout, a holiday meal or a larger event, we want the counter to be useful—not intimidating.

We source from trusted suppliers and carry a broad mix of beef, pork, poultry, sausage, seafood, prepared foods, seasonings and sauces. Special orders are part of the business because not every good meal fits neatly into a standard package.

Veteran-Owned & Operated

Service, accountability and taking care of the people around us are not marketing copy here. They are part of how the shop is run.

Our customers are the reason the shop has grown from a local butcher counter into an award-winning Gulf Coast destination. We appreciate every person who drives out to Tucker Road, asks a question, tries something new or brings us into an event.

Thank you for choosing The Butcher on Tucker—where quality, honor and community meet at the counter.
