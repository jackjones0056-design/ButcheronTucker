About Us

Welcome to The Butcher on Tucker

We are a veteran- and family-owned butcher shop committed to serving our community with excellent meats and dependable service.

Our team takes pride in the craft of butchery. The people behind the counter understand the products they sell and work to provide customers with consistently well-prepared cuts and helpful recommendations.

Quality remains the priority. We work with trusted suppliers and value the local relationships that help us bring dependable products from the source to your table.

The shop carries a broad range of meats for different tastes and occasions, including premium beef, poultry, pork, sides and specialty items. Customers can ask the butcher for recommendations, special cuts and help choosing the right product for a recipe or event.

We want the shop to earn long-term trust by serving the community with integrity and personal service.

As a veteran/family owned and operated business, we proudly support local veterans. The work ethic, accountability and service mindset veterans bring are part of the way we operate, and supporting the shop also supports veteran families in the community.

Whether you are a serious home cook, preparing a family meal or simply looking for quality meat and a friendly counter, The Butcher on Tucker is here to help.

Thank you for choosing The Butcher on Tucker, where quality, honor and community come together.
